How borrowing works

A librarian helps each book find its next reader.

  1. 01

    Choose a book

    Browse the catalog and send a request for an available title.

  2. 02

    Wait for approval

    The librarian reviews your request and confirms the loan details.

  3. 03

    Collect and return

    Pick up and return the book at the community location by its due date.
